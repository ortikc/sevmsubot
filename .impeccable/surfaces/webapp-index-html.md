# Surface: Telegram Mini App (webapp/)

Mode: Operate. Audience: students of МГУ Севастополь on phones inside Telegram. Task: see own group's pairs (now / next / today / week), first-run onboarding (направление → курс → группа → имя), profile.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The student's day read as a lighthouse's light characteristic: pairs are lit flashes, breaks are eclipses, the current pair is the one burning lamp. Refuses the category default of a flat list of equal cards with a blue accent.

OWN-WORLD: Night-sea navy ground (#0A1430 → #13234D → #21376E), porcelain-white text, a single warm lamp amber (#FFD27A) reserved for "now" and the selected day. Golos Text for all language, JetBrains Mono tabular figures for times and rooms. Elevation by tonal steps of navy, 14px radii, no glass, no gradients on text.

STORY: Opening the app, the student instantly sees which pair is lit now or next, where it is (room), who teaches it, and how long remains; one tap on the week strip shows another day's light pattern.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header: Telegram avatar, real name, group · course. Date heading. Day-light strip (time-proportional pairs, now-marker). Current/next pair card: subject heading, room and time in large mono, teacher and remaining minutes. Remaining pairs as a quiet time-column list. Bottom: week strip Пн–Сб, each day with ticks = pair count, today/selected lit.

FORM: Chersonesos lighthouse light characteristic, candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list; seed:4573665d9d857898. Raises: day tabs carry load ticks (acetate tab manual); strip length = real duration (labanotation); tabular mono time column (datamatics).
